About Me

I am a Statistician and Bioinformatician with a strong foundation in statistical theory and applications. Currently pursuing my PhD in Biostatistics at UTHealth Houston, I combine my expertise in statistical modeling with deep learning approaches to solve complex biological problems. My research focuses on developing novel methods for biomarker discovery and multi-omics data analysis, with a particular interest in graph neural networks and their applications in precision medicine.
