About Me

I am a Statistician, Data Scientist and Bioinformatician with a strong foundation in statistical theory and applications. Having recently defended my PhD in Biostatistics at UTHealth Houston (expected graduation: August 2025), I integrate statistical modeling with deep learning to address complex biological challenges. My research centers on developing novel methods for biomarker discovery and multi-omics data integration, with a particular focus on graph neural networks and their applications in precision medicine.
